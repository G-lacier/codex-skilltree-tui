# Technical brief

Identify the intended reader and the decision or finding the document needs to communicate. Describe the observed behavior and relevant constraints with enough context to assess the result.

Connect conclusions to supporting measurements, code references, or documentation. Distinguish established findings from hypotheses and record unresolved questions that could affect the decision.

For a proposed change, compare the relevant options and tradeoffs. For completed work, describe the resulting behavior and verification performed. End with any concrete follow-up needed to use or evaluate the result.
